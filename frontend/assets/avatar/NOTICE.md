# Strobi avatar

`strobi.avatar.json` is the "Strobi" avatar exported from
[Bible Strong Avatar Lab](https://github.com/smontlouis/bible-strong-avatar-lab) Studio
(same expressions and animations as `examples/react-vite-consumer/src/strobi.avatar.json`).

It is rendered at runtime by `@bible-strong/avatar-web@0.1.0` (and its dependency
`@bible-strong/avatar-core`), loaded from jsDelivr in `assets/site.js`.
