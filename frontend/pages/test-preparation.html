Which test?

Which test do I need?

Your universities decide. As a rule of thumb:

English-taught bachelor’s or master’s

An English proficiency test such as IELTS Academic or TOEFL iBT, unless you qualify for an exemption.

Master’s or PhD in the USA

Often the GRE, plus an English test. Many programmes have made the GRE optional, so check each one.

MBA or business master’s

The GMAT, or the GRE where accepted, plus an English test.

FAQs

Test preparation: common questions

When should I take my test?

Aim to have your score at least 2–3 months before your earliest application deadline, so there is time for a retake if needed.

Do I need a test if I studied in English?

Some universities waive English tests for students taught in English, but many do not, and visas can have their own rules. Check each university and your visa requirements.

Where do I book the exam?

You book directly with the test provider. Each test page links to the official booking site.
