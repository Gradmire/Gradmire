Quick answers

Planning to study abroad?

How long does a postgraduate course usually take?

Many international master’s programmes take one to two years. The exact duration depends on the country, university and course; check the relevant destination and course listing.

Can international students work part-time while studying?

Work permission and allowed hours depend on your visa, course and destination. Review the destination’s latest official rules before accepting work.

What documents are commonly needed for a student visa?

Typical items include a valid passport, admission confirmation, financial evidence, academic records and any required health, language or biometric documents. Requirements vary by destination and applicant.

What happens when I book a free consultation?

A counsellor reviews your study goals, profile and preferred destinations, then helps you understand course options and next steps.
