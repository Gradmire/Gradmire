01

What is always free

The free consultation is free. We never ask for card or bank details to book it, and booking does not commit you to any paid service.

Our online tools (Study Cost Calculator, Course Comparator, Deadline Tracker and grade converter) are also free to use.

02

Fees for paid services

Before any paid service starts, we give you a written quote that lists the service, what is included, the total fee including taxes, and when payment is due. We will not charge you without your written agreement to that quote, and there are no extra Gradmire charges beyond it.

Fees paid to others

Some costs are charged by other organisations, not by Gradmire. These include university application fees, tuition deposits, visa and immigration fees, biometric and visa-centre charges, English-test fees, translation, attestation and courier costs. You pay these directly to the organisation concerned, and their refund rules apply. Gradmire cannot refund them.
