Why Germany?

World-class education, near-zero tuition

Germany is one of the world's top study destinations — and uniquely, most public universities charge only a small semester contribution (€150–350) rather than tuition fees. Home to TU Munich, one of Europe's premier technical universities, Germany excels in engineering, sciences, and research. Indian students benefit from an 18-month post-study job search visa, a transparent visa process, and a booming economy hungry for skilled graduates.

Admission Requirements

Eligibility Criteria

Undergraduate
10+2 with 70%+ in relevant subjects. Many public universities require an APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) certificate — a document verification service for Indian students.
Postgraduate
Bachelor's degree (75%+ / 7.5 CGPA). Relevant undergraduate subject match required. German programs may require TOEFL/IELTS; international programs need English proficiency.
German Programs
German language proficiency B2–C1 (TestDaF, DSH, Goethe) required for German-taught programs. Start learning 12–18 months before your intended intake.
English Programs
IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL 90+ for English-taught master's programs. Germany has 1,000+ English-medium programs, especially at the master's level in STEM and business.
Immigration

German Student Visa Process

1
Receive Admission Letter + Complete APS
Get your university admission and complete the APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) verification — mandatory for Indian students. APS issues a certificate validating your academic documents.
2
Open a Blocked Account (Sperrkonto)
Deposit ₹10–11 lakh (€11,208) in an approved blocked account (Deutsche Bank, Expatrio, Coracle, etc.). The bank releases €934/month once you arrive — this is proof of financial means for the visa.
3
Apply at German Consulate / Embassy
Book a visa appointment at the German Mission in India (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bangalore, Hyderabad). Submit your admission letter, APS certificate, blocked account proof, health insurance, and accommodation documents.
4
Arrive and Register (Anmeldung)
Register your address at the local Bürgeramt within 14 days of arriving. Get your student ID from the university and register for health insurance through TK, AOK, or Barmer (mandatory for students).
5
Get Residence Permit (Aufenthaltserlaubnis)
Visit the local Ausländerbehörde to convert your entry visa to a residence permit for the duration of your studies. Bring registration certificate, insurance, enrollment proof, and passport photos.
6
Post-Study: 18-Month Job Seeker Visa
After graduation, apply for the 18-month job seeker visa. Once you secure a job matching your qualifications, switch to an EU Blue Card (long-term work permit). PR eligibility after 21–33 months of skilled work.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Public universities in Germany do not charge tuition for most programs — you pay only a semester contribution of €150–350 covering admin fees and often a public transport pass. Private universities and some professional master's programs charge €10,000–25,000/year. The cost-free model applies to both domestic and international students at public unis.
A blocked account (Sperrkonto) is a German bank account where you deposit proof-of-funds before applying for a student visa. The current requirement is €11,208/year (updated annually — check current rates). The account releases €934/month once you're in Germany. Providers include Deutsche Bank, Expatrio, Fintiba, and Coracle.

Working as a student assistant (HiWi) at your university is a popular and well-paying option. Working during semester is allowed but must not impact studies.
Not for your studies — but daily life in Germany (supermarkets, banks, authorities, local transport) still requires basic German, especially outside Berlin and Munich. We strongly recommend A2–B1 German before you arrive. Free German courses are often available at the university's language centre. Learning German also dramatically improves your job prospects post-graduation.
The APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) is mandatory for all Indian students applying to German universities. It verifies your 10+2 and bachelor's certificates. The process takes 4–8 weeks and costs around €75. You'll need to submit your documents in person at the APS India office in Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, or Bangalore. Start early — universities won't process applications without it.
