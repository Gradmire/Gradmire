Tests for 2027

IELTS, TOEFL, GRE and GMAT: your test plan for the 2027 intake

Tests are the first thing to book because everything else waits for your scores. This guide shows which tests apply, how long scores last and how to work back from your earliest deadline so you still have room for a retake.

Schedule

Work back from your first deadline

  1. Find your earliest deadline

    Look at your shortlist and note the first date, including scholarship deadlines that close before the course.

  2. Leave time for the score report

    Test providers need time to release and send scores. Check their current turnaround before you plan.

  3. Plan a retake

    If your earliest deadline is in mid-January, aim to have a first result by early December, so a second attempt still fits.

  4. Book early

    Popular winter dates fill up. Book your slot as soon as you know your window.

  5. Check validity at the visa stage

    Scores must be valid when you apply to the university and sometimes when you apply for the visa.

Visa

Make sure it is the right version of the test

Some visa routes accept only a specific, approved version of an English test. For example, certain UK visa routes need an approved secure English language test, and the university or the GOV.UK student visa page says whether yours does. Confirm this before you book.

Preparation

A simple preparation plan

  1. Take a diagnostic test to see where you stand on each section.
  2. Set a target score from your programmes’ requirements, not a general number.
  3. Practise every week and keep a log of mistakes by section.
  4. Sit full-length mock tests under real time limits.
  5. Get feedback on writing and speaking, where self-study is hardest.
  6. Decide your retake date before you sit the first test.
FAQ

Test questions for 2027

Should I take IELTS or TOEFL?

Both are widely accepted. Check which tests your shortlisted universities and visa route accept, then choose the format you are more comfortable with.

Do I need the GRE for 2027?

Only if your programmes ask for it. Many programmes make the GRE optional, so check each programme page before you spend months preparing.

Will a test taken now still be valid for autumn 2027?

IELTS and TOEFL scores are normally valid for two years, and GRE and GMAT scores for five, so a test taken this winter should still be valid. Confirm the rule at each university.

Can I retake a test?

Yes, tests can be retaken, subject to the provider’s rules. Plan the gap before your earliest deadline.
