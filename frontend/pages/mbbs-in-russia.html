MBBS in Russia

A long-established route into medicine, at a fraction of private-college fees.

Russia has trained international doctors for decades. Large state medical universities run English-taught General Medicine programmes with their own teaching hospitals, and annual fees stay well below most private MBBS seats in India.

Why Russia

What makes Russia worth considering

Established universities

State medical universities with large affiliated hospitals and long experience teaching international cohorts.

Lower annual fees

Tuition is typically far below private MBBS fees in India, and hostel costs are modest outside Moscow and St Petersburg.

Large Indian community

Most big medical cities have Indian student groups, Indian mess options and seniors who help newcomers settle in.

Know before you go: Patients speak Russian. Universities teach the language from year one, and you will need working Russian before clinical rotations, so treat it as part of the course, not an extra.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Basic eligibility for Indian students

  • NEET-UG qualified. The NMC requires a qualifying NEET score for any Indian student who plans to practise in India after an MBBS abroad.
  • Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English. At least 50% aggregate in PCB (40% for SC/ST/OBC, 45% for PwD candidates), or the university’s own higher cut-off.
  • Age 17 or older by 31 December of the admission year.
  • A valid passport and the documents the university and embassy ask for.

The NMC checklist every programme must pass

  1. The course runs at least 54 months, followed by a 12-month internship at the same university.
  2. The whole course is taught in English.
  3. The curriculum covers the subjects the NMC lists, with clinical training in the same country.
  4. The degree lets you register or get a licence to practise in that country, on the same terms as its own citizens.
  5. You finish the course within 10 years of joining.

Source: NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021

Student visa

Getting your Russia student visa

Indian students need a Russian study visa. The university applies for your official invitation; you then file the visa application with that invitation.

Typical visa file

  • Official invitation from the university (issued through Russia’s migration authorities)
  • Passport valid at least 18 months beyond entry
  • Visa application form and recent photographs
  • HIV-negative test certificate
  • Admission letter and proof of fee payment

After you arrive

The first visa is usually short. The university extends it after you arrive, then renews it every year of the course. You must register your address after you arrive.

Visa rules and document lists change. This is a planning summary, not legal advice. Always follow the current checklist from the embassy and your university.

Student life

Living in Russia as an Indian student

Climate

Long, cold winters, often well below zero in central and Siberian cities, so budget for proper winter clothing in year one.

Food

Hostel kitchens and Indian mess services are common in cities with large Indian cohorts.

Getting around

Metro, tram and bus networks are extensive and student passes keep travel affordable.

After your MBBS

Clinical training and careers after graduation

Practise in India

Pass India’s licensing exam (currently FMGE, to be replaced by NExT), then complete a 12-month supervised internship in India before full registration.

Practise abroad

Graduates can also prepare for other countries’ licensing routes, such as the USMLE (USA), the UKMLA/PLAB (UK) or the AMC exams (Australia), each with its own eligibility rules.

Postgraduate study

Specialisation options depend on where you are licensed. Plan early if you are aiming for PG in India or abroad.

FAQs

MBBS in Russia: common questions

Do I have to learn Russian if the course is in English?

Yes. Lectures are in English, but you will speak with patients in Russian during clinical years. Universities include Russian classes, and students who take them seriously from year one find rotations far easier.

Is there an entrance exam?

Most universities admit Indian students on Class 12 PCB marks and a qualifying NEET score. Some run a short interview or test, so check the university’s own admission rules.

Will my MBBS abroad be valid in India?

To practise in India you must meet the NMC’s Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021, pass India’s licensing exam (currently FMGE, to be replaced by NExT), and complete a 12-month supervised internship in India. Check every programme against the regulations before you apply.

Does the NMC publish a list of approved foreign universities?

No. The NMC does not approve individual foreign universities. It sets conditions the course must meet. A university appearing in the WHO/World Directory of Medical Schools is necessary but not enough on its own; the programme you join must also meet the NMC rules.

How long is my NEET score valid for studying abroad?

Under current NMC guidance, a qualifying NEET-UG result can be used for admission abroad for three years from the result date. Check the NMC website for the latest notice before you apply.
