Scores

Scoring and who accepts it

How it’s scored

Since 21 January 2026, each section and the overall score are reported on a 1–6 scale in half-band steps, aligned with CEFR levels. Score reports also show the familiar 0–120 scale during a transition period of about two years, so check which score your universities ask for.

Who accepts it

Universities across the USA and Canada, and many in the UK, Europe and Australia. Visa acceptance varies by country, so check before you rely on it for a visa.

FAQs

TOEFL: common questions

Can I take TOEFL at home?

Yes. The TOEFL iBT Home Edition has the same content, taken online with a remote proctor. Check that your universities accept it.

Is TOEFL or IELTS better?

Neither is easier for everyone. TOEFL is fully computer-based; IELTS has a face-to-face speaking test. We help you choose based on your universities and your strengths.

Where do I book the test?

Book directly with the test provider at ets.org/toefl. We help you pick a date that fits your application deadlines.
