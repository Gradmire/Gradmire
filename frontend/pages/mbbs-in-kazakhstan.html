Eligibility

Who can apply

Basic eligibility for Indian students

  • NEET-UG qualified. The NMC requires a qualifying NEET score for any Indian student who plans to practise in India after an MBBS abroad.
  • Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English. At least 50% aggregate in PCB (40% for SC/ST/OBC, 45% for PwD candidates), or the university’s own higher cut-off.
  • Age 17 or older by 31 December of the admission year.
  • A valid passport and the documents the university and embassy ask for.

The NMC checklist every programme must pass

  1. The course runs at least 54 months, followed by a 12-month internship at the same university.
  2. The whole course is taught in English.
  3. The curriculum covers the subjects the NMC lists, with clinical training in the same country.
  4. The degree lets you register or get a licence to practise in that country, on the same terms as its own citizens.
  5. You finish the course within 10 years of joining.

Source: NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021

Student visa

Getting your Kazakhstan student visa

Indian students apply for a Kazakh student visa on the strength of the university’s invitation.

Typical visa file

  • Invitation from the university (processed through Kazakh migration services)
  • Valid passport and photographs
  • Visa application form
  • Admission letter and fee receipt
  • Medical certificate, including HIV

After you arrive

Register with the migration police after arrival. The university usually arranges this and the annual extensions.

Visa rules and document lists change. This is a planning summary, not legal advice. Always follow the current checklist from the embassy and your university.

Student life

Living in Kazakhstan as an Indian student

Climate

Continental: hot summers and very cold winters, especially in Astana.

Food

Indian mess options exist at major universities; groceries are affordable.

Language

Russian is widely used in cities; Kazakh is the state language.

After your MBBS

Clinical training and careers after graduation

Practise in India

Pass India’s licensing exam (currently FMGE, to be replaced by NExT), then complete a 12-month supervised internship in India before full registration.

Practise abroad

Graduates can also prepare for other countries’ licensing routes, such as the USMLE (USA), the UKMLA/PLAB (UK) or the AMC exams (Australia), each with its own eligibility rules.

Postgraduate study

Specialisation options depend on where you are licensed. Plan early if you are aiming for PG in India or abroad.

FAQs

MBBS in Kazakhstan: common questions

Is the course really shorter in Kazakhstan?

Not any more. The NMC requires at least 54 months of study plus a 12-month internship, so choose a programme of about six years in total. Avoid any programme that cannot show this.

Are winters a problem?

Winters are harsh in the north. Almaty is milder; factor climate into your choice of city.

Will my MBBS abroad be valid in India?

To practise in India you must meet the NMC’s Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations, 2021, pass India’s licensing exam (currently FMGE, to be replaced by NExT), and complete a 12-month supervised internship in India. Check every programme against the regulations before you apply.

Does the NMC publish a list of approved foreign universities?

No. The NMC does not approve individual foreign universities. It sets conditions the course must meet. A university appearing in the WHO/World Directory of Medical Schools is necessary but not enough on its own; the programme you join must also meet the NMC rules.

How long is my NEET score valid for studying abroad?

Under current NMC guidance, a qualifying NEET-UG result can be used for admission abroad for three years from the result date. Check the NMC website for the latest notice before you apply.
