Scholarships in Germany

Scholarships to study in Germany

Public universities in Germany charge little or no tuition for most programmes (Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students), so scholarships in Germany mainly help with living costs.

DAAD Study Scholarships for master’s

Master’s

German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)

Value
A monthly stipend (about €934–€992), plus health insurance and travel allowances
Who can apply
International graduates applying for a master’s at a German university. Some programmes target specific fields or development-related courses (EPOS).
When to apply
Deadlines vary by programme and country, often autumn of the year before study.

FAQs

Scholarships in Germany: common questions

Are scholarships available for bachelor’s students?

Yes, but fewer than for master’s study. Most bachelor’s funding comes from universities as partial tuition discounts or need-based aid, so apply early and to several universities.

Do scholarships cover everything?

A few fully funded scholarships cover tuition, living costs and travel, but most are partial. Plan for the full cost of study and treat a scholarship as a bonus until it is confirmed.

Can I apply before I have an admission offer?

For some scholarships, yes; others need an offer first. Check each scholarship’s rules on its official page.
