Scores

Scoring and who accepts it

How it’s scored

The total score runs from 205 to 805, with a score for each section. You can review and change up to three answers per section, and choose the order of the sections.

Who accepts it

MBA, Master in Management, finance and business analytics programmes at business schools worldwide.

FAQs

GMAT: common questions

GMAT or GRE for an MBA?

Most business schools accept both. The GMAT is built for business school; the GRE suits applicants also considering non-business programmes. We help you choose.

Is there an essay?

No. The GMAT Focus Edition has no essay section.

Where do I book the test?

Book directly with the test provider at mba.com. We help you pick a date that fits your application deadlines.
