Why Italy?

Ancient universities, ultra-affordable tuition, and unmatched culture

Italy hosts the world's oldest university — the University of Bologna, founded in 1088. Public Italian universities charge just €900–4,000 per year in tuition for international students, making Italy one of Europe's most affordable destinations. Italy excels in design (Politecnico di Milano is one of Europe's top design schools), architecture, fashion, art history, and engineering. With over 600 English-taught master's programs and a growing startup scene in Milan and Rome, Italy offers more than heritage — it's a modern, competitive EU destination.

Private universities like Bocconi charge €13,000–16,000. Milan is the most expensive city; Bologna, Turin, and Naples are significantly cheaper.

Funding Your Studies

Scholarships for Indian Students

🏛️
MAECI Italian Government Scholarship
Scholarships offered by the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs through the Italian Cultural Institute. Covers full tuition, monthly allowance of €900, and accommodation assistance. Available for bachelor's, master's, PhD, and research programs. Apply through the Indian embassy.
Full tuition + €900/month · Annual selection · Apply via Italian Cultural Institute
🎓
DSU Regional Scholarships (Income-Based)
Italy's regional DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) grants provide means-tested support — cash bursaries, free meals, and reduced accommodation — for students demonstrating financial need. Almost all public university students qualify to apply. Even with tuition reductions, many students effectively study for free.
Up to full tuition waiver + €2,000–5,000 bursary · Income-based
🏆
Politecnico di Milano — Merit Award
Merit scholarship for high-achieving international students admitted to master's programs. Assessed automatically upon admission. Covers full tuition waiver for outstanding applicants, or partial reduction for others.
Full or partial tuition waiver · Design, Engineering, Architecture programs

Admission Requirements

Eligibility Criteria

Undergraduate
10+2 with 70%+ from a recognized board. A Declaration of Value (DoV) — a document certifying your qualifications' equivalence — is required and issued by the Italian Embassy in India. Apply for it early (8–12 weeks processing).
Postgraduate
Bachelor's degree with 60%+ from a recognized Indian university. Declaration of Value (DoV) required for the visa. Some competitive programs (engineering at Politecnico, MBA at Bocconi) are more selective — 75%+ recommended.
English Programs
IELTS 6.0–6.5 or TOEFL 80+ for English-taught programs. Politecnico di Milano has its own English test for some programs. Italian-taught programs require B2 Italian (CILS or equivalent).
PhD / Research
Master's degree (or equivalent). Competitive selection with research proposal and supervisor contact recommended. Many Italian PhD programs come with a monthly stipend of €1,000–1,200 — effectively paid positions.
Immigration

Italian Student Visa Process

1
Pre-enrollment via Universitaly Portal
Register on the Universitaly portal (universitaly.it) and submit your pre-enrollment application to your chosen university. This step is required before applying for an Italian student visa. Some universities have separate direct admission portals — check your university's website.
2
Obtain Declaration of Value (DoV)
Apply for a Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore) from the Italian Embassy or Consulate in India. This document certifies that your Indian qualifications are equivalent to Italian standards. Processing takes 8–12 weeks — start early. Fee is approximately ₹2,500–4,000.
3
Apply for Type D Study Visa
Apply at the Italian Consulate (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, or Kolkata). Documents: offer letter, pre-enrollment confirmation, DoV, bank statements (€5,000+ per year), accommodation proof, travel insurance, and passport. Processing takes 3–6 weeks. Visa fee: €50.
4
Arrive and Register (Permesso di Soggiorno)
Within 8 days of arriving in Italy, apply for a Permesso di Soggiorno (residence permit for study) at the post office (Poste Italiane) — submit the kit form available there. You'll get an appointment at the local Questura police station. This permit allows you to stay and study for the academic year.
5
Post-Study: Job Seeker or Work Permit
After graduation, Italy offers a 1-year post-study job search visa for graduates from Italian universities. Once you secure a job, your employer applies for an EU Blue Card or skilled work permit. Long-term residency (permesso CE) after 5 years of legal Italian residence — a pathway to EU permanent residence.
