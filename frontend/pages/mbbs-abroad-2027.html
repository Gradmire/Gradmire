MBBS abroad in 2027

MBBS abroad in 2027: eligibility, NEET and how to plan

Studying medicine abroad is a licensing decision as much as an admissions one. This guide covers who can apply, what India’s National Medical Commission looks for, how long programmes run in each country we cover, and the red flags that cost students years.

Gradmire guideReviewed 10 November 20266 min read

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • A qualifying NEET score. Indian applicants need one to study medicine abroad. Confirm the NEET UG 2027 schedule and rules on the official NTA website.
  • School-leaving marks in biology and chemistry. Most universities admit on these, and some also run a short interview or test. Check each university’s own admission rules.
  • Age and documents. Requirements differ by university and country, so confirm them on the university’s international admissions page.

Check before you pay anything. Dates, fees and visa rules change. We last reviewed this guide on 10 November 2026. Confirm every date and requirement on the official source before you apply or pay anything.

Licensing

What India’s licensing body looks for

If you plan to practise in India, the NMC Foreign Medical Graduate Licentiate Regulations 2021 apply. As summarised in our country guides:

  • A programme of about six years in total, with at least 54 months of study plus a 12-month internship.
  • The internship done at the same university where you studied.
  • A university listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools, teaching an authorised programme.
  • Clinical training in hospitals, not only classroom teaching.

Plan

A planning timeline for 2027

  1. Now to January 2027: research

    Shortlist countries and universities. Check each against the NMC rules and the World Directory of Medical Schools.

  2. Before NEET UG 2027: prepare

    NEET is normally held in the first half of the year. Confirm the dates and registration window with NTA.

  3. After results: apply

    Apply to your shortlisted universities. Many MBBS programmes abroad start in autumn, and some have more than one intake.

  4. Offer to visa

    Accept one offer, pay only what the offer letter says, and apply for the student visa through the official route.

  5. Before you fly

    Confirm accommodation, health cover and how the 12-month internship will be done at the university.

Stay safe

Red flags when you plan MBBS abroad

  • Admission is promised without NEET.
  • The university is not in the World Directory of Medical Schools, or the programme is not the English-taught one authorised for international students.
  • The internship cannot be done at the same university.
  • A very low fee is the only selling point. Compare clinical training, not just price.
  • You are asked to send money before you have an official offer letter.
FAQ

MBBS abroad questions for 2027

Do I need NEET to study MBBS abroad?

Indian applicants need a qualifying NEET score. Confirm the current rule on the NTA and NMC websites.

Which country is best for MBBS abroad?

It depends on recognition, programme length, teaching language, the internship and your budget. Compare the country guides and verify each university before you apply.

Can I do the internship in India?

Usually not. Many licensing bodies, including India’s NMC, require the internship at the same university where you studied. Confirm the university offers it before you enrol.
