Overview

Why study in the UK?

The United Kingdom is home to some of the world's most prestigious universities — Oxford, Cambridge, UCL and Imperial consistently rank among the top 10 globally. A UK degree is internationally recognised and typically takes 3 years for a Bachelor's and 1 year for a Master's, saving both time and money compared to many other destinations.

Post-Brexit, Indian students remain the second-largest international cohort in the UK. The Graduate Immigration Route (the "Post-Study Work" visa) lets you live and work in the UK for 2 years after graduation — 3 years for PhD graduates.

The diverse, multicultural campus environment, world-class research output, and strong alumni networks make the UK a compelling choice for ambitious Indian students.

Application deadlines by programme type

MBA
Oct–Apr · rolling rounds
Most UK business schools run 4–5 intake rounds. Apply in Round 1 (Oct–Nov) for best scholarship consideration. Final rounds close around April.
MSc (taught postgraduate)
Apply Jan–March for Sep intake
Most programmes open applications in October and expect you to apply by January–March. Some close earlier once scholarship funding is allocated.
Undergraduate (UCAS)
15 January · 15 October for Oxbridge
Standard UCAS deadline: 15 January. Oxford, Cambridge, and all Medicine/Dentistry courses require applications by 15 October — no exceptions.
Heads up on competitive programmes: some highly competitive MSc programmes fill their cohort well before the official deadline. If you're targeting Oxford, Cambridge or a ranked business school, apply before December and have all materials ready.
Entry Requirements

Eligibility criteria

Undergraduate (UG)
Bachelor's Degree
  • Class XII with 70–85% (science/commerce/arts depending on course)
  • IELTS: 6.0–7.0 overall (no band below 5.5)
  • UCAS points equivalent: 96–144 (varies by university)
  • SAT / AP considered for top universities
Postgraduate (PG)
Master's / MBA
  • Bachelor's degree: 55–70% (varies by course and university)
  • IELTS: 6.5–7.5 overall (common: 6.5)
  • GRE/GMAT required for MBA and some STEM MSc programmes
  • 1–2 years work experience (preferred for MBA)
Research (PhD)
PhD / MPhil / MRes
  • Master's degree with a Merit or Distinction (60%+)
  • IELTS: 6.5–7.0 overall
  • Research proposal and supervisor contact required
  • Published work or strong research portfolio is a plus
Pre-University
Foundation / Diploma
  • Class X pass with 55%+ for Foundation Year
  • IELTS: 5.5 overall
  • Bridge to first-year undergraduate at partner university
  • Age requirement: 17+ at start of programme
ATAS clearance for CS/AI and Engineering applicants: some computer science, AI/ML, advanced engineering, aerospace, and certain materials science courses require Academic Technology Approval Scheme (ATAS) clearance before your visa is issued. This can add 4–6 weeks to your timeline — plan for it early. Your Gradmire counsellor will confirm whether your specific course needs it.

Student Visa

UK Student visa process

The UK Student visa (formerly Tier 4) is applied for online. Gradmire's visa counsellors guide you through every step.

01
Receive Unconditional Offer + CAS
Your university issues a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) number once you hold an unconditional offer. The CAS is required to apply for the Student visa.
02
Complete Online Application
Apply on GOV.UK up to 6 months before your course starts. Fee is £363. International health surcharge (IHS) of ~£776/yr also applies.
03
Biometrics & Document Submission
Book a VFS appointment in India for biometric enrolment and document submission. Core docs: CAS, financial evidence, English test, passport.
04
Visa Decision
Standard processing: 3 weeks. Priority service (£500 extra) for 5 days. Apply at least 6–8 weeks before travel to be safe.
05
Arrive & Collect BRP
On arrival, collect your Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) from your university or nearest post office within 10 days. This is your official proof of right to study.
06
Apply for Graduate Route on Completion
After graduation, switch to the Graduate Immigration Route to live and work in the UK for 2 years (3 for PhD). No job offer needed to apply.
