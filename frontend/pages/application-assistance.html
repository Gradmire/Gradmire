Process

    We look at your academics, test scores, experience and goals.

Documents

What you’ll usually need

Undergraduate applications

  • Class 10 and 12 (or equivalent) mark sheets
  • English test score, unless exempt
  • Personal statement
  • One or two references
  • Passport copy

Postgraduate applications

  • Degree transcripts and certificates
  • English test score; GRE or GMAT where required
  • Statement of purpose
  • Two or three references
  • CV, plus portfolio or work experience letters where relevant

FAQs

Application assistance: common questions

Will you write my SOP for me?

No. Admissions teams want your own voice, and many check for authenticity. We help you plan, structure and improve your own writing.

How many universities should I apply to?

Usually five to eight, balanced between ambitious, target and safer choices. The right number depends on the country and course.

When should I start?

Ideally 9–12 months before your intended start date, so there is time for tests, documents and early deadlines.
