Visas by country

What each destination asks for

A summary of the main student visa routes. Requirements depend on your nationality and change often, so always use the official checklist.

United Kingdom

Student visa

CAS from your university, proof of funds held for 28 days, TB test where required, and the Immigration Health Surcharge.

Official guidanceStudy in United Kingdom

United States

F-1 student visa

Form I-20 from your university, SEVIS fee, DS-160 form and a visa interview at the US embassy or consulate.

Official guidanceStudy in United States

Canada

Study permit

Letter of acceptance, a provincial or territorial attestation letter where required, proof of funds and biometrics.

Official guidanceStudy in Canada

Australia

Student visa (subclass 500)

Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), Genuine Student requirement, proof of funds, English test and Overseas Student Health Cover.

Official guidanceStudy in Australia

Germany

National (D) visa for study

Admission letter, blocked account or other proof of funds, health insurance and, for some nationalities, an APS certificate.

Official guidanceStudy in Germany

Ireland

Study visa (Stamp 2)

Letter of acceptance, proof of fees paid, evidence of funds and private medical insurance.

Official guidanceStudy in Ireland

New Zealand

Student visa

Offer of place, proof of tuition payment and living funds, plus health and character requirements.

Official guidanceStudy in New Zealand

Italy

Type D study visa

Pre-enrolment through Universitaly, proof of funds and accommodation, and health insurance.

Official guidanceStudy in Italy

Finland

Residence permit for studies

Admission letter, proof of funds, insurance meeting Migri’s rules, and an online application through Enter Finland.

Official guidanceStudy in Finland

Avoid refusals

Common reasons applications go wrong

Unclear funds

Funds that don’t meet the required amount or holding period, or large unexplained deposits.

Inconsistent details

Dates, names or study plans that don’t match across documents and forms.

Weak study plan

Not being able to explain why this course, this university and this country fit your goals.

Late applications

Applying too close to your course start, leaving no time for delays or extra checks.

FAQs

Visa assistance: common questions

When should I apply for my visa?

As soon as you have the document your destination requires (such as a CAS, I-20, CoE or letter of acceptance) and your funds are ready. Processing times vary by country and season.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No one can. Decisions are made by the immigration authorities. We help you submit a complete, consistent application and prepare well for any interview.

I had a visa refused before. Can you help?

Yes. We review the refusal reasons and help you address them before you reapply.
