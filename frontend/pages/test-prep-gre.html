Test format

What the GRE tests

Graduate Record Examinations (General Test)

SectionTimeWhat it covers
Analytical Writing30 minOne “Analyze an Issue” essay.
Verbal Reasoning41 minTwo sections, 27 questions: reading comprehension, text completion and sentence equivalence.
Quantitative Reasoning47 minTwo sections, 27 questions: arithmetic, algebra, geometry and data analysis. An on-screen calculator is provided.
Scores

Scoring and who accepts it

How it’s scored

Verbal and Quantitative are each scored 130–170 in 1-point steps; Analytical Writing is scored 0–6 in half-point steps. With ScoreSelect you choose which test dates to send.

Who accepts it

Master’s and PhD programmes worldwide, especially in the USA, and a growing number of business schools as an alternative to the GMAT.

FAQs

GRE: common questions

Do all master’s programmes need the GRE?

No. Many have made it optional, but a strong score can still help, especially for competitive STEM programmes or scholarships.

How long should I prepare?

Most students prepare for 6–12 weeks, depending on their starting score and target.

Where do I book the test?

Book directly with the test provider at ets.org/gre. We help you pick a date that fits your application deadlines.
