About Gradmire

Two descriptions you can use

Short

Gradmire is a study-abroad counselling company for students in India. It offers a free first consultation and fee-only counselling with no commissions from universities, plus free planning tools, from offices in Kanpur and Greater Noida.

Longer

Gradmire is a study-abroad counselling company for students in India. It offers a free first consultation and fee-only counselling with no commissions from universities, plus free planning tools, from offices in Kanpur and Greater Noida. Gradmire Education Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2026 in Noida by Aquib Jamal. It publishes guides for nine study destinations and nine MBBS countries, scholarship and student-visa guides, and four free tools: a study cost calculator, a course comparator, a deadline tracker and a GPA, CGPA and SGPA converter. Where a figure or rule can change, the guide links to the official source.
