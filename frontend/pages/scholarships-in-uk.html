Scholarships in UK

Scholarships to study in United Kingdom

UK funding for international students is mostly for master’s study, from the UK government, the British Council and individual universities. Bachelor’s scholarships exist but are usually partial and set by each university.

Chevening Scholarships

Master’s

UK government (FCDO)

Value
Full tuition, a monthly living allowance, return flights and visa costs
Who can apply
Future leaders from Chevening-eligible countries (including India) with at least two years of work experience, for a one-year master’s at any UK university.
When to apply
Applications usually open in August and close in early November, a year before study.

Commonwealth Master’s Scholarships

Master’s

Commonwealth Scholarship Commission

Value
Tuition, airfare and a living allowance
Who can apply
Citizens of eligible low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries, for master’s study in development-related subjects. Some countries apply through a national nominating body.
When to apply
Usually opens in the autumn, with deadlines set by each country’s nominating route.

GREAT Scholarships

Master’s

British Council and UK universities

Value
At least £10,000 towards tuition
Who can apply
Students from selected countries (India is one) with an offer for a one-year taught master’s at a participating university. Participating universities change every year.
When to apply
Usually announced early in the year, with university deadlines in spring.

Gates Cambridge Scholarships

Master’s

Gates Cambridge Trust

Value
Full cost of study at Cambridge, including a maintenance allowance
Who can apply
Outstanding applicants from outside the UK applying for a full-time postgraduate degree, including some master’s courses, at the University of Cambridge.
When to apply
Applied for through the Cambridge course application, usually by October or December.

Clarendon Scholarships

Master’s

University of Oxford

Value
Full course fees and a grant for living expenses
Who can apply
Academically excellent graduate applicants to Oxford, including master’s courses. You are considered automatically when you apply by the relevant deadline.
When to apply
Considered with your Oxford application, usually by the December or January deadline.

FAQs

Scholarships in UK: common questions

Are scholarships available for bachelor’s students?

Yes, but fewer than for master’s study. Most bachelor’s funding comes from universities as partial tuition discounts or need-based aid, so apply early and to several universities.

Do scholarships cover everything?

A few fully funded scholarships cover tuition, living costs and travel, but most are partial. Plan for the full cost of study and treat a scholarship as a bonus until it is confirmed.

Can I apply before I have an admission offer?

For some scholarships, yes; others need an offer first. Check each scholarship’s rules on its official page.
