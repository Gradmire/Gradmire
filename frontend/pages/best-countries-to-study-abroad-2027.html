Choosing a country

Best countries to study abroad in 2027 for Indian students

There is no single best country, only the best country for your course, budget and plans after graduation. Here is how to compare the nine destinations we cover, using questions you can answer before you apply.

Gradmire guideReviewed 10 November 20267 min read

How to compare

Seven questions that decide the best country

We do not rank countries. Rankings move with exchange rates, visa rules and your own profile. Answer these instead.

  1. What will the whole degree cost, including living costs, not just yearly tuition?
  2. How long is the course? A one-year master’s changes the total.
  3. What funding can you realistically win, and when do those scholarships close?
  4. Which language will you study and live in?
  5. Which intake can you meet with your tests and documents?
  6. What are the work rules during and after study, today?
  7. How predictable is the visa process for your course level and funds?

Country notes

What to know about each destination for 2027

UK

One-year master’s degrees are common and UCAS handles undergraduate applications. September is the main intake. Read the Graduate route rules before you plan work after study. UK guide

USA

Widest choice, with many graduate assistantships. Fall is the main intake. Check student work rules on the official SEVIS page. USA guide

Canada

Study permit limits and provincial rules affect how many applications are accepted, so confirm the current rules with IRCC before you pay a deposit. Canada guide

Australia

Two main intakes, February and July, and the subclass 500 student visa. Compare costs city by city. Australia guide

Germany

Winter semester is the main start. Indian applicants are normally asked for an APS certificate, which can take months, so confirm the current rule with DAAD or your university. Germany guide

Ireland

One-year master’s degrees are common and September is the main intake. Check the registration steps after you arrive. Ireland guide

New Zealand

Two main intakes, February and July, and a small set of universities to compare. New Zealand guide

Italy

Autumn start and regional scholarships. Many non-EU applicants complete a pre-enrolment step, so check the dates for your course. Italy guide

Finland

English-taught degrees use national application windows on Studyinfo, so check the window for your programme. Finland guide

FAQ

Choosing a country: common questions

Which country is cheapest to study in?

It depends on course length, tuition, city and funding. A country with high yearly fees but a one-year master’s can cost less than a cheaper country with a two-year degree. Compare the total, not the yearly fee.

Which country has the easiest student visa?

No country guarantees a visa. Outcomes depend on your documents, funds and course. Be careful with anyone who promises approval.

Which countries offer the most scholarships?

Every destination we cover has some, from government awards to university merit awards. See our scholarships for 2027 guide and the scholarship page for each country.

Can I work while I study?

Most of these countries allow limited work during study and have separate rules after graduation. The rules change, so read the official government page for your country before you count on income.
