Study abroad intakes in 2027: when to apply for each country

An intake is a start date for a course. Pick the wrong one and you can miss scholarships or wait a year. Here is how the main 2027 intakes compare, and what to do if you are already late for the first one.

Why it matters

Three things change with the intake

Choice of courses

The main intake usually has the widest range of courses. Smaller intakes may offer only some programmes, often at master’s level.

Scholarships

Many scholarship rounds are tied to the main intake. Early-year intakes can have fewer awards, so ask before you apply.

Timing and housing

Visa processing, deposits and accommodation all follow the intake date. A later intake gives you more time to prepare.

Dates

Dates to watch for 2027 entry

Most deadlines are set by each university, so the only fixed dates we list are the UCAS ones. For everything else, open the programme page and write the deadline down.

  • UK undergraduate (UCAS): 15 October 2026 for Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses (already passed), and 18:00 UK time on 13 January 2027 for all other undergraduate courses.
  • UK master’s: you apply to each university directly. Many programmes fill as they go, so apply early.
  • USA: undergraduate colleges often have early and regular rounds, and many master’s programmes set deadlines in winter for fall entry. Check each programme.
  • Canada, Ireland, Germany, Italy and Finland: deadlines for autumn entry usually fall in winter and spring, and some countries add steps such as document evaluation. Start those steps first.
  • Australia and New Zealand: February intakes close earlier than July intakes, so check which one you can still meet.

Source: UCAS 2027 entry deadline, checked on 10 November 2026.

Running late?

If you missed an early 2027 deadline

  • Ask for late or rolling admission. Some programmes accept applications until seats are full. Email admissions with your profile.
  • Move to the next intake. A strong application for autumn 2027 beats a rushed one for January.
  • Look at pathway or foundation routes if your marks or English score are below the direct-entry level.
  • Use the time. Finish your test, build your scholarship list and prepare funds.
  • Do not pay for “guaranteed” admission. No agent can guarantee an offer or a visa.
FAQ

Intake questions, answered

What does “fall 2027 intake” mean?

Fall is the American and Canadian word for the autumn start, usually August or September 2027. It is the main intake for most universities in those countries.

Is the January 2027 intake still open?

For many programmes the January 2027 window has closed or is closing. A few universities accept late applications, so ask admissions directly before you give up on it.

Which intake has the most scholarships?

Autumn usually has the most scholarship rounds, but some universities also fund January starters. Check the scholarship page of each university.

Can I apply for more than one intake?

Often yes, but each university has its own rules about changing or deferring an application. Read the programme page or ask admissions.
