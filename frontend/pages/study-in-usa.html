Overview

Why study in the USA?

The United States hosts the world's largest international student population — over 1 million students from 200+ countries. Home to 9 of the world's top 10 universities (QS), a US degree carries unmatched global prestige, particularly in STEM, business, and research.

US universities offer unparalleled flexibility: choose your major, switch programmes, access need-based financial aid, and customise your academic journey like nowhere else. Research funding and campus facilities are in a league of their own.

Post-graduation, STEM OPT gives graduates 3 years to work in the US before transitioning to an H-1B, making it one of the best career launchpads globally for Indian engineers, scientists, and business professionals.

Student Visa

F-1 Student visa process

The F-1 is the most common US student visa. Here's how Indian students apply through the US Embassy in New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, or Kolkata.

01
Receive I-20 from university
Once accepted, the university issues a Form I-20 (Certificate of Eligibility). This document is required to apply for the F-1 visa and register with SEVIS.
02
Pay SEVIS Fee (I-901)
Pay the $350 SEVIS fee online at fmjfee.com. Keep the receipt — you'll need it for your visa interview.
03
Complete DS-160 Online Application
Fill out the DS-160 form online at ceac.state.gov. Pay the visa application fee ($185). Schedule your interview at the nearest US Consulate.
04
Attend Visa Interview
Bring: passport, I-20, SEVIS receipt, DS-160 confirmation, financial proof, offer letter, and academic transcripts. Show strong ties to India and clear study intent.
05
Apply for OPT After Graduation
Apply for 1-year Optional Practical Training (OPT) after completing your degree. STEM graduates can extend for 2 additional years (total 3 years) to gain US work experience.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

F-1 visa approval rates for Indian students are generally high (80–90%) when you have a legitimate acceptance from a recognised university, sufficient financial proof, and clear academic intent. Gradmire counsellors prep you thoroughly for the visa interview.
STEM OPT is a 24-month extension of the standard 12-month OPT available to graduates in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Maths fields. Combined, STEM graduates can work in the US for 3 years on F-1 status, giving them time to find an employer willing to sponsor an H-1B visa.
Yes — particularly for PhD and some Master's programmes, funding through Teaching Assistantships (TA) and Research Assistantships (RA) is common. Many universities waive tuition and provide stipends for PhD students. At the undergrad level, need-based aid from top schools (MIT, Yale, Princeton) can cover 70–100% of costs.
The H-1B is a US work visa for speciality occupations. After OPT, if your employer sponsors you, you can apply for H-1B, which is issued in a lottery (annual cap of 85,000). The wait and lottery makes it uncertain, which is why many Indian graduates also consider other immigration routes to permanent residency.
