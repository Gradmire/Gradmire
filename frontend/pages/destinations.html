How to compare study destinations

A destination suits you when the course, the cost, the entry requirements and the options after your degree all work together. Look at these before you choose, and use the tools to check your own numbers.

The course first

Some countries are stronger or cheaper for particular subjects, so compare programmes, not just country names. Search the Course Finder.

The full cost

Add tuition, living costs and test fees for the whole programme, then set the total against your budget and expected earnings. Use the cost calculator.

Entry requirements

Check academic, English-test and other requirements early so your shortlist is realistic. Compare courses side by side.

Scholarships and funding

Scholarships have their own eligibility rules and deadlines, and many are set by individual universities. See scholarships by country.

Visa, work rights and deadlines

Check student visa rules, limits on part-time work and what is allowed after you graduate, then track every deadline. Track your deadlines or read about visa assistance.

Not sure which country is right for you?

Our specialist counsellors help you compare costs, rankings, work rights and visa pathways — free of charge.
