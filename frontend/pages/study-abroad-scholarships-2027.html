Scholarships for 2027

Study abroad scholarships for the 2027 intake

Most scholarship deadlines for autumn 2027 fall between this winter and next spring, and some flagship awards have already closed. Here is where funding comes from, which programmes to look at by country, and how to build a plan that does not depend on a single award.

Funding sources

Where scholarship money comes from

Government scholarships

National programmes such as Chevening (UK), DAAD (Germany) and Australia Awards. Competitive, with fixed yearly deadlines.

University scholarships

Merit and international-student awards from the university itself. Many are decided with your course application.

Need-based aid and assistantships

US universities offer need-based aid, and graduate departments offer assistantships with a tuition waiver or stipend.

Indian trusts and foundations

For example the Inlaks Shivdasani Foundation, the J N Tata Endowment and the K C Mahindra Education Trust. Several are loan scholarships, so read the terms.

Plan

Build a scholarship plan in six steps

  1. List 10 to 15 options

    Mix government awards, university awards and Indian trusts, so no single decision decides your funding.

  2. Check eligibility first

    Read nationality, degree level, subject and work-experience rules. Drop what you cannot meet.

  3. Write the deadlines down

    Many university awards close with the course application, and flagship awards close months before the intake.

  4. Prepare the common documents

    Transcripts, CV, statement of purpose, recommendation letters and test scores serve most applications.

  5. Tailor each statement

    Selection panels read many essays. Explain why this award and this course fit your plan.

  6. Track everything

    Use the deadline tracker so no date slips past.

Stay safe

How to spot a scholarship scam

  • You are asked to pay a fee to be “selected” or to “release” a scholarship.
  • You get a message that says you won something you never applied for.
  • The site is not the provider’s official domain, or the link comes from a private message.
  • The offer guarantees an award or a visa.
  • Always open the scholarship from the provider’s official website, and keep your passport and bank details off chat apps.
FAQ

Scholarship questions for 2027

Can I get a full scholarship?

Some awards cover full tuition and living costs, but they are highly competitive. Plan for partial funding and keep a backup budget.

Are there still scholarships open for 2027?

Yes. Many university awards open with the application for the course, so they are still available while you can apply. Some national awards for 2027 have closed, so note when they open again.

Do I apply for the scholarship separately?

Often yes, but some universities consider you automatically when you apply. The scholarship page says which.

Does Gradmire take money for scholarships?

No. Scholarship applications go directly to the provider. We do not earn commission from universities or scholarship providers.
