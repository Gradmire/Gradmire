FAQs

Course selection: common questions

Should I choose the course or the country first?

Usually the course. Your subject and career goals narrow down the countries and universities that suit you, and the destination follows.

Are rankings important?

They are one signal. Course content, accreditation, location, cost and graduate outcomes often matter more for your career.

Can I switch fields for a master’s?

Often, yes. Some master’s programmes are designed for career changers, while others need a related degree. We help you find realistic options.
