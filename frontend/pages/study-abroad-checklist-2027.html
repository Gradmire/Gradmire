Study abroad checklist for 2027: documents and your next 30 days

Documents, not ideas, are what delay applications. Transcripts, recommendation letters and bank papers take weeks. Start with the next 30 days below, then use the document list to check nothing is missing.

Gradmire guideReviewed 10 November 20266 min read

Start today

Your next 30 days

  1. Write down your plan: course, two or three countries, intake and total budget.
  2. Book your English test (and the GRE or GMAT if your programme needs it).
  3. Ask two or three referees for recommendation letters, and send them your CV and plans.
  4. Order marksheets, transcripts and your degree certificate. These can take weeks from your institution.
  5. Check your passport: validity, and that your name matches your certificates.
  6. Build a shortlist of six to ten courses and record each deadline in the deadline tracker.
  7. Outline your statement of purpose and talk with your family about funding.
Documents

The document checklist

What you need depends on the country and course, so treat this as the common set and confirm the rest on each programme page.

Identity

  • Passport with enough validity
  • Passport-size photographs to each country’s specification
  • Aadhaar or other ID if the portal asks

Academic

  • Class 10 and 12 marksheets
  • University transcripts and degree or provisional certificate
  • Medium-of-instruction letter, if requested

Tests

  • IELTS, TOEFL or another accepted English test
  • GRE or GMAT, only if the programme asks

Application

  • CV
  • Statement of purpose
  • Recommendation letters
  • Portfolio or work samples, if relevant

Financial

  • Bank statements and income proof
  • Sponsor documents
  • Education loan sanction letter, if used
  • Scholarship award letters

Visa and travel

  • Offer letter and the visa document the university issues
  • Health insurance
  • Medical or police certificates, when required
Avoid delays

Mistakes that delay applications

  • Name or date mismatches between passport, marksheets and application forms.
  • An expiring passport. Many visas need validity beyond the course end.
  • Assuming requirements are the same everywhere. Each course sets its own tests and documents.
  • Relying on one deadline. Apply to a spread of courses and intakes.
  • Leaving funding papers to the end. Loans and bank letters take time.
  • Paying for “guaranteed” admission. Nobody can promise an offer or a visa.

FAQ

Document questions, answered

How early should I start collecting documents?

Now. Transcripts, certificates and recommendation letters often take weeks, and some deadlines fall in winter.

Do I need attested or translated documents?

Some countries and universities ask for attested copies or translations of certificates. The programme page or visa page says which.

How many recommendation letters do I need?

It varies by programme. Many ask for two or three. Check the requirement before you ask your referees.

Where do I upload documents for the visa?

On the official visa portal of the country you are going to. Never send your documents through unofficial links.
