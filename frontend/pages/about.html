Our story

Study abroad, without the guesswork.

Gradmire was founded in 2026 in Noida by Aquib Jamal, who believes that choosing where to study abroad is one of the biggest decisions a student and their family will ever make. The advice behind it should be clear, honest and built around the student.

For too many families, the process is confusing and expensive: scattered information, unclear costs, and advice that doesn't always put the student first. Gradmire brings university search, course matching, scholarships and one-to-one counselling into one place, so you can compare your options and apply with confidence.

Today we guide students online, wherever they are in India, with branches across Delhi/NCR, Greater Noida, Lucknow and Kanpur. We're a new company, and we'd rather earn your trust than claim it. Every recommendation is based on your goals, your budget and your profile.
