Meet the founder

Where you study is a big decision. Aquib Jamal built Gradmire to make it a clear one.

Choosing where to study abroad is one of the biggest decisions a student and their family will ever make. Aquib Jamal founded Gradmire in 2026 on one belief: the advice behind that decision should be clear, honest and built around the student.

For too many families, the process is confusing and expensive: scattered information, unclear costs, and advice that doesn't always put the student first. Gradmire exists to change that, by bringing university search, course matching, scholarships and one-to-one counselling into one place.

So Gradmire works differently. It is fee-only, with no commissions from universities, which means the only person a counsellor works for is you. Each counsellor focuses on a set of destinations and goes deep on them, and every recommendation is checked against your goals, your budget and your profile.

Gradmire is a new company, and it would rather earn your trust than claim it. Come and meet the team at our offices in Kanpur and Greater Noida, or book a free first consultation online from anywhere in India.
