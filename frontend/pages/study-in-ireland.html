Why Ireland?

English-speaking EU — tech giants on your doorstep

Ireland punches far above its weight as a study destination. Home to the European headquarters of Google, Meta, Apple, LinkedIn, Pfizer, and Goldman Sachs, Ireland gives graduates unparalleled access to world-class employers in a compact, English-speaking EU country. Trinity College Dublin is Ireland's premier university with a history dating to 1592. A 2-year graduate stay-back permit allows graduates to work and transition to employment permits.

Admission Requirements

Eligibility Criteria

Undergraduate
10+2 with 75%+ in relevant subjects. Some programs (medicine, dentistry) require additional entrance exams (HPAT-Ireland). Portfolio for design, architecture courses.
Postgraduate
Bachelor's degree with minimum 60–65% (2:1 equivalent). Some programs require work experience or professional references. Science and engineering need relevant UG background.
IELTS / English
IELTS 6.5 overall (6.0 in each band) for most programs. Trinity, UCD require 7.0+ for competitive courses. Indian students from English-medium institutions may qualify for waivers.
PhD / Research
First-class or high honours bachelor's or master's degree. Research proposal required. Contact potential supervisors directly before applying. Funding from Science Foundation Ireland available.
Immigration

Ireland Student Visa Process

1
Receive Offer Letter from CORU/ISD Recognized Institution
Secure your unconditional offer from an Irish institution listed on the ILEP (International Education Programme). Pay your first-year fees and secure accommodation — both are often required for the visa application.
2
Apply for Irish Study Visa (Type D)
Apply online at AVATS (Automated Visa Application Tracking System). Gather documents: offer letter, fee payment receipt, bank statements (€7,000+), travel insurance, passport-sized photos, and cover letter explaining your study plans.
3
Biometric Appointment at VFS Ireland Centre
Submit your documents and biometrics at the VFS Ireland Visa Application Centre in your city. Visa processing typically takes 6–8 weeks. Apply at least 8 weeks before your course start date.
4
Register with GNIB / IRP on Arrival
Within 90 days of arriving, register at the Garda National Immigration Bureau (GNIB) in Dublin or local Garda station elsewhere. You'll receive a Stamp 2 — your official student residence permit. Renewal annually.
5
Graduate Stay-Back: Third Level Graduate Scheme
On completing your degree: degree holders get 1 year, honours degree or master's get 2 years on Stamp 1G to seek employment. Once you find a job (€32,000+ salary), your employer applies for a General Employment Permit or Critical Skills Employment Permit.

Yes — students on Stamp 2 can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and 40 hours per week during vacation periods (June 1–September 30 and December 15–January 15). Ireland's minimum wage is €12.70/hour (2024). Campus jobs, retail, and hospitality are common options.
Ireland offers something unique — it's English-speaking and part of the EU. Unlike the UK (post-Brexit), an Irish work permit can lead to EU freedom of movement. Many global companies use their Irish offices as their European headquarters, creating abundant opportunities for international graduates. For those wanting EU access plus English language, Ireland is increasingly popular.
The Critical Skills Employment Permit (CSEP) is the most favourable Irish work permit for high-skilled occupations — tech, engineering, healthcare, finance. Salary threshold is €50,000+ (€38,000+ for some IT roles). CSEP holders can bring dependents who can work freely, and are eligible for long-term residency (Stamp 4) after 2 years — a faster PR route than most countries.
