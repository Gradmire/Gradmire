Study abroad in 2027: the plan for Indian students

If you want to start a degree abroad in 2027, the decisions that matter most are happening now: which intake to aim for, which tests to book and which countries to shortlist. This guide puts them in order, starting from November 2026.

The short version

What to know before you start

Most 2027 starts are in autumn

For the UK, USA, Canada, Ireland, Germany, Italy and Finland the main start is usually September or October 2027. Australia and New Zealand start in February and July.

Some early-2027 intakes are closing now

January starts in the UK, USA and Canada, and February starts in Australia and New Zealand, often have application windows that close around now. Ask each university whether it still accepts applications.

Work backwards from the deadline

Tests come first because scores take time to arrive. Then shortlist, documents, applications, funding and the visa. The plan below shows the order.

Check before you rely on this. Dates, fees and visa rules change. We last reviewed this guide on 10 November 2026. Confirm every date and requirement on the official source before you apply or pay anything.

Timeline

A month-by-month plan from November 2026

Use this as a skeleton and move the dates to match your own universities. The detailed document list is in the 2027 checklist.

  1. November to December 2026: decide and test

    Fix your budget, pick two or three countries and book your English test, plus the GRE or GMAT if your course asks for it. Ask your referees for recommendation letters early.

  2. January to February 2027: shortlist and apply early

    Finish a shortlist of courses and universities. Submit applications for any early-2027 deadlines and early autumn rounds. Draft your statement of purpose and get it reviewed.

  3. March to April 2027: apply and fund

    Submit your remaining applications. Apply for university scholarships alongside the course, because many close with the application. Prepare proof of funds and any education loan paperwork.

  4. May to June 2027: offers and decisions

    Compare offers on cost, course content and scholarships. Accept one offer, pay the deposit it asks for, and request the visa document your country uses, for example a UK CAS, a US I-20 or an Australian CoE.

  5. June to August 2027: visa and travel

    Apply for your student visa as soon as the university issues its documents. Book insurance, accommodation and flights once your visa position is clear.

  6. September 2027: arrive and settle

    Attend orientation, complete any registration your university or the local authorities need, and set up your bank account and phone.

UK undergraduate applicants: UCAS lists 18:00 UK time on 13 January 2027 as the main deadline for 2027 entry. Oxford, Cambridge, medicine, dentistry and veterinary courses had an earlier deadline of 15 October 2026. Check your course on UCAS.

Budget

Build your 2027 budget before you apply

We do not quote tuition or visa fees here because they change every year. Use the university and government pages for the numbers, and cover these items.

  • Tuition for the whole course, not only year one.
  • Living costs in the city you will actually live in.
  • Health cover and visa charges as listed by the official visa page.
  • One-off costs: deposit, accommodation advance, flights and first-month setup.
  • A buffer for the rupee moving against the pound, dollar or euro.

FAQ

Questions about studying abroad in 2027

Is it too late to apply for 2027?

No, not for the autumn 2027 intake, which is the main intake in most countries. The January and February 2027 starts are the ones closing now, so ask each university whether it still accepts applications.

Which intake is best for 2027?

Autumn usually has the widest choice of courses and the most scholarship rounds. Early-year intakes can have fewer seats and less funding but let you start sooner. The right answer depends on your tests, documents and budget.

When should I take IELTS or TOEFL for 2027?

Early enough to retake before your first deadline. IELTS and TOEFL scores are normally valid for two years, so a test taken this winter should still be valid for autumn 2027 entry. Confirm each university’s rule.

Do I need an agent or consultant to apply?

No, you can apply directly. A counsellor helps you compare options and avoid mistakes. Gradmire does not earn commission from universities, so our advice does not depend on which university you choose.

Will visa rules change before 2027?

They can. Countries change student visa and work rules often, so treat any rule you read, including here, as a starting point and check the official government page before you apply.
