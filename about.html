Our story

18 years of getting students to their dream universities.

Gradmire was founded in 2006 by a group of former international students who knew first-hand how confusing and costly the study-abroad process was for Indian families.

What started as a single office in Mumbai — helping 30 students in our first year — has grown into India's most trusted study-abroad counselling platform, with 24 offices across India and counsellors who collectively hold over 400 years of experience.

We've never taken a commission from universities or charged inflated fees. Every rupee in your admission offer stays in your pocket. Our only incentive is your success.
