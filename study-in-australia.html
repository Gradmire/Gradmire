Overview

Why study in Australia?

Australia is the third-largest international student destination in the world, and for good reason. Featuring 7 universities in the global top 100 (QS), a safe multicultural society, and a stunning natural environment, Australia combines academic excellence with lifestyle appeal unmatched elsewhere.

Australia's Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485) allows international graduates to live and work in Australia for 2–4 years after graduation — longer for regional universities. This provides a gateway to skilled migration pathways and permanent residency.

Australia leads globally in mining engineering, environmental science, veterinary science, and nursing. Business and IT graduates also find robust employment in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane's growing tech sectors.

Student Visa

Australia Student visa (subclass 500)

01
Receive Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)
Pay your first-semester fees (or initial deposit) to trigger your CoE from the university. You need this electronic document to apply for the visa.
02
Apply Online via ImmiAccount
Apply at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. Fee: AUD $710. Processing time: 4–6 weeks for most Indian applicants.
03
Health Examination (HAP)
Book a mandatory health examination at an approved panel physician in India. Results go directly to the Department of Home Affairs.
04
Biometrics at VFS
Submit biometrics at the nearest VFS Global centre. Documents required: CoE, financial proof, English proficiency, passport, health check.
05
Apply for Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)
After graduating, apply for the 485 visa to stay and work in Australia for 2 years (Bachelor's/Master's) or 4 years (PhD). No job offer needed.
