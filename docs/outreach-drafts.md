# Outreach drafts

Prepared 10 November 2026. Ready-to-edit emails for the targets in `docs/backlink-outreach-plan.md`. **Nothing here has been sent.** Each one needs a real name, a real page and a first line that shows you read the other site.

## Before you send any of them

- Open the page you are linking to and check every claim on it against its official source.
- Personalise the first sentence. If you cannot say something true and specific about their page, do not send.
- One email per target. Send at most one follow-up, 7 to 10 days later. Never mass-mail.
- Never offer money, a swap or a review in return for a link. If a placement is paid it must carry `rel="sponsored"`.
- Log each send in `docs/backlink-tracker.csv`.
- Use a named person's email address, not a shared inbox, where you can.
