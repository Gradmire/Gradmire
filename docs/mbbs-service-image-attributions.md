# MBBS, student services and scholarships image attributions

The photos on the MBBS Abroad, Student Services and Scholarships pages are locally stored, web-optimised derivatives of freely licensed files on Wikimedia Commons. Each file was resized (960 px and 640 px wide) and recompressed to WebP; nothing was added to or removed from the pictures. Only CC0, public domain, CC BY and CC BY-SA files are used (never NC or ND). CC BY and CC BY-SA files are credited on the public [Photo credits](../frontend/pages/photo-credits.html) page, which is built from this table by `scripts/build-credits-page.mjs`. CC BY-SA terms also apply to the resized copies.

Pictures without prominent faces were chosen on purpose: a free copyright licence does not cover a person's likeness, so people are kept out of promotional images.
