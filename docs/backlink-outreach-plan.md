# Backlink outreach plan

Prepared 2 November 2026. A **backlink** is a link from another website to Gradmire. It cannot be added from inside this site; it has to be earned or requested. This plan lists what to fix first, what to offer, and who to ask, in the order that is most likely to work for a new site.

**Principles**

- Ask only where the link helps that site's readers. Relevance beats volume.
- Every claim on the pages you point people to must be true and sourced (`docs/rules.md` §3.4). Outreach puts those pages in front of editors, universities and journalists, who will check.
- Never buy links, join link exchanges, use blog networks, post links in comment sections, or write reviews of yourselves. Google treats these as link spam. If a placement is paid or sponsored, it must carry `rel="sponsored"`.

## 0. Fix first (before any outreach)

| Item | Why it matters | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm the "no commission" promise or soften it | Editors and universities will read the homepage and About page | `index.html`, `about.html`, `faq.html` |
| Add a real business phone number | Directory and Google profiles need Name, Address and Phone to match the website exactly | Contact page, footer |
| Settle one story for address and founding | The site says founded in Noida in 2026, with two offices: Kanpur (Near Halim College, Prem Nagar) and Greater Noida (N2 Tower, Supertech Ecovillage 1). Profiles must say the same thing everywhere | `about.html`, footer, `contact.html` |
| List only the two real offices | Kanpur and Greater Noida. Do not list any other branch in directories | Directory listings |
| Create the real profiles, then add them to the schema | The homepage structured data has no `sameAs` links. Add each profile URL once it exists | `index.html` JSON-LD |
| Put real counsellors on the About page, with consent | Journalists and credential programmes need named, real people. The placeholder team was removed | `about.html` |

## 5. Expert commentary and digital PR (month 2 onward)

Journalists look for quotable experts on student visas, costs, loans and policy changes. Answer only where you genuinely know the topic, using a named counsellor with a short bio and credentials.

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Featured.com](https://www.featured.com/) | Took over the HARO service in April 2025. Pricing and terms change, so check them first. |
| [Qwoted](https://www.qwoted.com/) | Expert and journalist matching. Paid tiers exist. |
| [SourceBottle](https://www.sourcebottle.com/) | Free expert requests |
| Source of Sources | Free HARO-style list. Its site blocked my automated check, so verify it manually. |

Good angles: when the UK, US, Canada or Australia change a student rule, publish a dated explainer for students from India with the official source linked, and offer a counsellor's comment to education reporters.

## 6. Universities, student societies and ambassadors (months 1 to 3)

- **Student ambassadors.** The new [Careers page](https://www.gradmire.com/career) recruits them. An ambassador can ask their own university's Indian student society or international office whether the grade converter or cost guide belongs on a resources page. That is a genuine reason for a link.
- **Pre-arrival and "resources for Indian students" pages.** Many university international offices keep these. Pitch the one tool or guide that fits, not the whole site.
- **Broken-link outreach.** Scholarship and visa resource pages often link to pages that no longer exist. If you set up the Ahrefs integration, it can list dead links on those pages, and you can offer the matching Gradmire page as a replacement.
- **Guest articles.** Write for education sites that have an editor. Use a named author with credentials. Do not pay for placement.

## 7. Outreach templates

Keep each message short, specific and honest. Never quote statistics you cannot source.

**Resource page**

> Subject: A free GPA converter for your Indian students page
>
> Hello [name], I saw that [page URL] lists resources for students from India. We run a free CGPA/SGPA/GPA converter at [URL]. It gives an indicative estimate and says clearly that each university sets its own rules. If you think it would help your students, I would be glad if you added it. Either way, thank you for the page. [Name, role, Gradmire]

**Expert commentary**

> Subject: Comment for your piece on [topic]
>
> Hello [name], I am [name], a study-abroad counsellor at Gradmire in India. On your query about [topic]: [two or three precise sentences, with the official source linked]. I am happy to be quoted with my name and role, or to answer follow-ups. [Credentials]

**Broken link**

> Subject: Broken link on [page title]
>
> Hello [name], the link to [dead URL] on [page URL] no longer works. If it helps, [URL] covers the same ground and is kept up to date. Thank you for maintaining the page.
