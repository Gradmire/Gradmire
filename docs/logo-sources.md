# Landing-page logo sources

Logo files used by the landing-page sliders in `frontend/pages/index.html` live in `frontend/assets/logos/`. Each was downloaded on 2026-10-16 from the file page linked below and committed unmodified. SVGs were checked for scripts, event handlers and external references before they were added.

Every logo is a trademark of its owner, shown only to name the institution or employer. Several files are marked "non-free / fair use" on English Wikipedia. Before launch, the owner should confirm Gradmire has permission to display them, or replace them with files from each organization's own brand or press kit.

## Content to verify

- **Top recruiters:** the section describes these companies as global employers that hire international graduates in Gradmire's destination countries. It does **not** say they recruited Gradmire students. If the business wants to make that claim, it needs evidence (see `rules.md` §3.4).

## Licence review (2 November 2026)

Each source file page was checked on Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons.

- **Removed:** `toronto.svg`, `melbourne.svg`, `nus.svg`, `edinburgh.svg` and `capgemini.svg`. They were English Wikipedia non-free "fair use" files, which is a rationale for Wikipedia articles only. Their cards on the homepage now show the institution name as text. To bring a logo back, take it from the organization's own brand or press kit and keep written permission.
- **Kept:** the other 15 files are marked public domain or "too simple to copyright" on Commons, but every one is still a registered mark (Commons tags them as trademarked). They may name the institution or employer only. Do not use them to suggest a partnership, endorsement or that Gradmire placed students there.
